What it is

A startup in a short video

Founders record short vertical videos about their startups and publish them to the feed. Every project has its own page with its pitches, a Brief and the team. Investors watch the feed and the project rating, mark their interest and message founders directly.

The app is Russian-first, with a full English version chosen by your device language. The demo content is in Russian.

  • Startup founders and teamsRecord and publish pitches, keep versions, collect feedback and see interest analytics.
  • Investors and business angelsDiscover projects through the feed, the rating and analytics, mark interest and message founders. 18+ only, with a passport check.
  • Anyone curious about startupsWatch pitches, follow, like and comment for free.

Features

What the app does

01

Video-pitch feed

A vertical feed of short video pitches with Following and Recommended tabs, likes, comments, shares and #hashtags.

02

Project pages

Every startup gets a page with its pitches and versions, a Brief (stage, team, category, ask), prototype links and the team.

03

Project analytics

Views, likes, comments and average watch time over 7 or 30 days, plus the audience by role. Creators see their own projects; investors see every project.

04

Project rating

A project rating built on audience interest: overall, growth and new, filterable by category. Available to investors.

05

Direct chats

Investors message founders and each other, founders message each other and their audience, and observers message each other. Either side can reply in an existing chat.

06

Explore and search

Search people, projects and videos, and browse popular hashtags.

About payment

  • Subscriptions are bought only through Google Play, inside the app. There are no card forms on the site or in the app.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Google Play shows the price in your currency, and it may differ by country.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically. You can cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Creators and investors cannot switch to each other; only an observer can change roles.
  • When a creator’s subscription lapses, their projects and videos stay visible, but creating and editing are blocked until renewal. An investor without an active subscription loses analytics and the rating.
  • During closed testing, purchases in the app are simulated: no money is charged.

How it works

Four steps

  1. Sign up and choose a role

    Observer is free. Creator and Investor are subscriptions through Google Play.

  2. Watch the feed

    Swipe through pitches in the Following and Recommended tabs, and follow projects and people.

  3. Publish a pitch

    A creator sets up a project, fills in the Brief, records a pitch and releases new versions.

  4. Mark interest and message

    An investor checks the rating and analytics, taps Interested, then Message, and continues in the chat.

Status

Coming soon to Google Play

The Android app is in closed testing. The test build runs in a demo mode:

  • its projects, people, videos and numbers are demo data and fictional;
  • purchases are simulated (“Test store”), and no real payments are made;
  • your account, profile and chats stay on the device and are not sent to any Pitch.me server.

Important

Pitch.me is not an investment platform

  • Pitch.me does not offer, sell or broker investments, and no money moves through the app.
  • The Ask on a project page is informational and marked “Not an offer”.
  • The Interested and Invested marks are signals only; no money moves.
  • The investor check is an 18+ age check by passport, not an assessment of experience or accreditation.
