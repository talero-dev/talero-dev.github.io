Pitch.me is currently available only in closed testing and runs in demo mode. In the demo build your account, profile, videos, chats and all other data you enter stay on your device and are not sent to any Pitch.me server. The current test build includes these third-party Google services: Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent), plus Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push-notification token). See section 7 and section 16. Sections 3–15 also describe the full service with the Pitch.me server, which has not launched yet. We will update this policy before it does.

1. Summary

  • Pitch.me is a social network for startup founders: a vertical video-pitch feed, projects, profiles, direct messages, and the paid Creator and Investor roles. The Observer role is free.
  • To sign up you need a phone number, a username, a first and last name and a password. E-mail is optional.
  • Other users see your profile, videos, projects and comments. Chat messages are seen only by the people in the chat.
  • The Investor role requires a passport check (18+ only). Documents are kept in private storage and deleted within 30 days of the decision (7 days after a rejection).
  • Payments go only through Google Play (and the App Store if an iOS version is released). We never receive card details.
  • Only Observers see Google AdMob ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads follow the consent you give in Google's form.
  • We use Google services: Google AdMob and Google UMP (ads and consent), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). All of them are also in the current test build.
  • We do not sell data for money. Under the laws of some US states, sharing data with Google for personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out in Settings → Ad settings (see section 12).
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account). In the demo build this erases the demo data on the device at once. Instructions and an e-mail address for deletion requests are at https://talero-dev.github.io/pitchme/delete-account/#en.

4. How we use data

  • To create and run your account, confirm your phone number, and handle login and password reset.
  • To show your profile, videos and projects to other users and to build the feed, search and recommendations.
  • To deliver direct messages and notifications.
  • To verify the identity and age of people who want to become Investors.
  • To activate and verify subscriptions and keep accounting records.
  • To show creators and investors statistics and project ratings, in aggregated form only, without showing who watched what.
  • To show ads to Observers and cap how often they appear.
  • To protect the service: rate limiting, abuse detection, handling reports, and an audit log of administrator actions.
  • To find and fix bugs using crash reports.
  • To understand how the app is used (Google Analytics for Firebase, automatic events only) so we can improve it.

We do not send marketing messages.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Project ratings and recommendations are automated but do not affect your rights; a person reviews every passport check.

7. Third parties: processors and independent controllers

We do not sell data for money. Under the laws of some US states, sharing data with Google for personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out in Settings → Ad settings (see section 12).

We share data only with the providers below. Processors act on our instructions and receive data only as far as each needs for its function. Google acts as an independent controller for AdMob data. Where a provider has not been chosen yet, it is described by type; we will name it in this policy before the full service launches.

7.1 Google Play Billing

Handles subscription payments. You enter payment details only in Google Play; we receive a transaction ID or purchase token and verify it with Google. Google Play sends subscription status notifications to our server. Google Play Billing is not called in the demo build. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7.2 Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

Included in the current test build; crash reporting is on in every release build. A crash or error report contains: stack trace, device model, OS version, screen orientation, RAM and free disk space, app version, Firebase installation ID, technical labels (platform, app version, demo-mode flag) and app log entries at warning level and above. Only the entry's level, the component name and the error type are sent from the log, never the message text; message text is also stripped from the errors the app reports itself. A report about an app crash may still contain the text of the system error message. Crashlytics is not used for advertising. The purpose is to find and fix crashes and keep the app stable and secure; the legal basis is our legitimate interests (see section 5). Firebase processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7.3 Google Analytics for Firebase

Included in the current test build. Only automatic usage statistics are collected: the app-instance ID; automatic events such as first open, session start, screen view, app or OS update and app exception; device model, OS, language, and approximate location derived from the IP address. The app sends no events of its own to Analytics and does not link this data to your account. Google Analytics does not collect the Android Advertising ID: its collection is turned off in the app's settings.

Consent (Consent Mode v2): by default, before consent is checked, the app denies analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation. After the Google UMP form, the app passes your choice to Analytics. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is turned on only if you consent to storing and accessing information on the device, and the ad signals only if you consent to the matching ad purposes and to Google as an ad vendor; elsewhere, the signals are turned on when Google allows ads in your region, except that after a US-state opt-out of sale or sharing, ad user data and ad personalisation stay denied. Without consent, Analytics does not store or read identifiers on the device and does not use data for ads; Google may still receive event information without identifiers. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad settings.

Google keeps Analytics data for up to 14 months, linked to the app-instance ID rather than to your account. The legal basis is in section 5. Firebase processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7.4 Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)

Included in the current test build: the app registers with FCM and gets a device token for push notifications; Google receives the token and the Firebase installation ID. In demo mode the token is kept only on the device and nobody sends push notifications, because there is no Pitch.me server in the demo. In the full service the Pitch.me server sends push notifications through FCM. Notification text (for example, about a new like or message) passes through Google; a new-message notification may include the start of the message. Firebase processes this data on our behalf.

7.5 Google AdMob

Shows ads in the Observer feed. Google may collect the Android Advertising ID and app set ID, device and app information (model, OS, app version, language, time zone), approximate location derived from the IP address (city or region, never GPS), and ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics. Google uses this to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

The ads SDK starts every time the app starts, for every user, including before sign-in and for Creators and Investors, although only Observers see ads. Google may therefore receive device and app information from every user.

Google acts as an independent controller for AdMob data under the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms. More:

8. Ads and consent

  • Only Observers see ads. Creator and Investor subscribers see no ads. The consent check and the ads SDK still start for every user (see 7.5).
  • Ads appear in the feed between videos. In the current test build they are Google AdMob ads; our own ads will come with the full service.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. AdMob ads store and read information on the device, so here they follow the consent you give in the Google UMP form. If you refuse, Google may show limited ads without identifiers.
  • Personalised and non-personalised ads. Personalised ads are chosen using the Advertising ID and other data Google collects. Non-personalised ads use only context (such as approximate location and the app).
  • US states. Where the law provides for it, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of data for personalised advertising (see section 12).
  • To change your choice: in the app, open Settings → Ad settings (shown when consent or an opt-out applies in your region).
  • Consent and statistics. The app also passes your choice in the Google UMP form to Google Analytics for Firebase (Consent Mode v2, see 7.3).
  • The Android Advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads. Once deleted, apps receive zeros instead of the ID.
  • Our own ads (full service). For these we record impressions, clicks and hidden ads to cap how often an ad appears. Ads are chosen by your role, country, platform and the topics and hashtags of videos you watched in the last 30 days. Advertisers receive aggregated statistics only, never details about individual users.
  • Pitch.me is not directed at children. The maximum ad content rating in AdMob is T (teen and older). Because the service is only for people aged 16 and over, we do not tag ad requests as coming from children.

10. Account deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete account, then confirm with your password.

On the web: https://talero-dev.github.io/pitchme/delete-account/#en gives instructions and an e-mail address for requesting deletion without reinstalling the app. E-mail talero.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Pitch.me: account deletion".

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Demo build (now): once you confirm with your password, the demo data is erased from the device at once, with no 14-day period: the account, profile, projects, chats, purchases and other changes made in the demo, the device ID and the login session. The app shows "Account and demo data erased" and returns to the Welcome screen (details in section 16). We hold no copy of demo data, so we can answer an e-mail request only with these instructions.

Full service (the 14-day period applies only here):

  1. After the request, the account is pending deletion for 14 days. Your projects are hidden and your other login sessions end. If you log in with your password during those 14 days, the deletion is cancelled.
  2. After 14 days we delete: your phone number, e-mail, first and last name, headline, bio, city, profile photo, the date of birth in your profile and your password; your device records; the projects you own (for every team member) and your videos with all their files; passport-check documents; uploaded files and export archives. All sessions end.
  3. The following are kept, linked to an anonymised "Deleted user" record without your name, phone or e-mail:
    • your username, which is not released for new sign-ups;
    • your comments, which are hidden from other users;
    • likes, follows, shares and blocks;
    • investor marks, including the amount and note;
    • consent records, and the deletion reason if you gave one;
    • messages you sent, which the other person still sees, without your name, until they delete the chat;
    • the passport-check outcome: decision, document country and encrypted date of birth;
    • search history for up to 1 year, notifications for 90 days, reports you filed for 1 year after the case is closed (see section 9);
    • detailed usage events and ad impressions, until the periods in section 9 (180 days and 13 months);
    • payment records, for 7 years for accounting;
    • aggregated statistics that cannot identify you;
    • data in backups, for up to 30 days.

If we shorten this list before the full service launches, we will update this section and the account-deletion page.

14. Security

  • Data travels only over encrypted HTTPS connections; unencrypted connections are disabled in release builds.
  • In the full service, passwords are stored only as a hash (Argon2id). In the demo build, the password is also stored on the device only as a salted hash, never in plain text (see section 16).
  • Phone numbers are stored as a keyed hash, e-mail addresses encrypted (AES-256-GCM), and IP addresses only hashed or masked.
  • Passport-check documents are kept in private storage. Reviewers open them through links valid for 10 minutes, and every view is logged.
  • Staff see contact details only in masked form, and their actions are logged.
  • Backups are encrypted.
  • In the demo build, data is kept in the app's private storage on the device, which other apps cannot read. It is not additionally encrypted. It is excluded from Google cloud backups and from direct transfers to a new phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

The hosting country of the Pitch.me server has not been chosen yet; we will name it here before the full service launches. When data leaves the EEA, the UK or Brazil, we use the safeguards the law provides, such as standard contractual clauses.

16. Demo mode and test builds

Closed-testing builds run in demo mode, with a built-in test server inside the app:

  • Nothing is sent to Pitch.me servers. Your account, profile, videos, projects, comments, chats, searches, settings and purchases are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • The feed is filled with sample content and fictional users. Chat replies are generated on the device.
  • Purchases are simulated: no money is charged and Google Play Billing is not called.
  • Videos, photos and documents you pick are not uploaded; the app only refers to the file on your device. The passport photo is not copied or sent, and the check is approved automatically.
  • No SMS or e-mail is sent. There are no push notifications: the app gets a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, but nobody sends notifications in demo mode.
